Invented brands start at zero. Geographic domains start with a place people already search for.

## Intent is pre-loaded

When someone types *Albuquerque waxing* or *spray tan ABQ*, the geography is the query. A hostname that contains the geography does not have to earn relevance from scratch — it inherits it from the phrase residents already use.

Invented names reverse the work: every mention has to carry a subtitle, a logo, and a "what do they do?" until the brand catches up.

## Three properties of a strong geo domain

1. **The phrase is stable.** City names outlast slogan cycles. Albuquerque was the market yesterday and will be the market next decade.
2. **The code is shared.** ABQ compresses city-wide identity into three letters — airport, weather, local shorthand — without narrowing the category.
3. **The length is printable.** Seventeen characters fit on a van wrap, a window decal, and a maps pin. Short geo names survive every channel.
